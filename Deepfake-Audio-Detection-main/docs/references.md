# References consulted

- [Media Security Lab: Audio Deepfake Detection resources](https://github.com/media-sec-lab/Audio-Deepfake-Detection) — curated bibliography and resource catalog, including dataset, feature, model, and training references. It is useful for discovery; it is not itself a runnable detector implementation.
- [ASVspoof 2019](https://www.asvspoof.org/index2019.html) — official challenge page and data access information for Logical Access speech synthesis/voice-conversion evaluation.
- [ASVspoof 2021](https://www.asvspoof.org/index2021.html) — official benchmark page, including a compressed speech deepfake track that can inform an out-of-domain/codec evaluation.
- Müller et al., [Does Audio Deepfake Detection Generalize?](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2022/muller22_interspeech.html), Interspeech 2022 — evaluates generalization beyond benchmark-style data and motivates keeping external-source performance separate from in-domain scores.
- Müller et al., [Harder or Different? Understanding Generalization of Audio Deepfake Detection](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2024/muller24b_interspeech.html), Interspeech 2024 — studies the generalization challenge and cautions that simply increasing model capacity may not solve it.

These references inform evaluation design. They do not imply that this project has reproduced their models or results.
